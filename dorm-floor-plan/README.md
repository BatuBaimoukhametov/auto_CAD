# Dorm Floor Plan (MKV Suite)

A CAD drawing of my dorm suite layout, drafted in AutoCAD ahead of moving in.

## Why I made this

Before moving into residence, I wanted a clear picture of the suite layout so I
could plan out furniture placement, figure out where my own stuff would fit,
and generally visualize the space before actually seeing it in person. I'd
normally have just sketched this by hand, but I used it as an opportunity to
do it properly in AutoCAD instead.

## What's in this repo

- `drawings/dorm_floor_plan.dxf` — the editable AutoCAD drawing (layers for
  walls, doors, furniture, and labels).
- `exports/dorm_floor_plan.pdf` — a clean, dimensioned export of the plan.

## Notes on the drawing

- The suite has four bedrooms, a shared living/dining area, a kitchen, and
  two bathrooms.
- Bedroom dimensions (3.3 m x 2.4 m) are based on measurements provided by
  the residence; the rest of the layout is estimated proportionally from a
  reference floor plan image and refined by eye.
- Drawn to scale with proper layer separation (walls, doors, furniture,
  dimensions) rather than as a flat sketch, so it can be extended later —
  e.g. laying out furniture more precisely once I'm actually living there.

## Tools

- AutoCAD (drafting, layers, dimensioning)
